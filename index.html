Blue pottery

Made without clay: quartz, glass and fuller's earth, hand-painted in cobalt and fired once at low heat. Each flower is drawn freehand, so no two pieces repeat.

Bastar, Chhattisgarh

Dhokra brass

4,000 years oldLost-wax casting

Every piece starts as a wax thread wound by hand over a clay core, then cast in molten brass. The mould is broken to free it, so each one is the only one.

Terracotta

River clayWood-fired kilns

Thrown on the wheel from Damodar river clay, burnished by hand and fired in open kilns until it rings. Earthy, breathable, and better with age.

Lacquerware

Ivory-wood latheVegetable dyes

Turned on a lathe from soft ivory-wood and coloured with lac sticks pressed against the spinning wood. Safe for small hands, bright for a lifetime.

Black pottery

Smoke-fired blackSilver etching

How the concierge works

Less searching. More finding.

01 · ASKSay it the way you'd say it to a shopkeeper

"Something calm and blue for the living room, under three thousand." The concierge pulls out craft, colour, room, occasion and budget, and shows you what it understood so you can correct it.

02 · WATCHIt learns from what you linger on

Pieces you hover, open, like or add quietly reshape your taste profile. Recent signals count more than old ones, and you can see every one of them.

03 · CHOOSEClassic store, one switch away

Turn the concierge off and the store is a normal catalogue: filters, sort, search. Turn it back on and your shortlist is waiting.

In production the concierge reads requests with a language model, ranks with product embeddings and live behaviour, and respects consent. This mockup runs a small on-device matcher so it works offline.
